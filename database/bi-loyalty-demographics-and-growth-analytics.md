# 📊 Business Intelligence: Loyalty Architecture, Demographics & Growth Analytics

Today, I explored enterprise loyalty BI architecture, participated in a deep-dive session on Qlik dashboarding, and engineered demographic and customer acquisition analytics.

### 🚀 Key Technical Deliverables
* **Loyalty BI Architecture:** Attended an architectural review session examining data flows, semantic layers, and dashboard pipelines in enterprise loyalty and rewards systems.
* **Month-over-Month (MoM) Customer Delta:** Computed percentage variance in active customer bases across consecutive months to monitor growth and retention trends.
* **Demographic Revenue Contribution:** Segmented customer populations into dynamic age brackets and calculated the proportional revenue share (`SUM(revenue_by_age_bracket) / Total Revenue * 100`) per demographic group.
* **Acquisition & Conversion Metrics:** Calculated overall purchasing customer conversion ratios and determined daily average new member acquisition velocities.

### 💡 Insight
Segmenting revenue by age brackets combined with MoM retention tracking provides actionable demographic insights for targeted campaign strategies, while separating raw transaction volume from unique member conversion rates prevents overestimating customer base engagement.
