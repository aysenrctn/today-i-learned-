# 🗄️ SQL Analytics: Customer Segmentation, Campaign Analysis & Daily Metrics

Today, I engineered complex multi-dimensional SQL queries to analyze customer transaction activity, company-level breakdowns, campaign touchpoints, and daily operational throughput.

### 🚀 Key Technical Deliverables
* **Activity & Inactivity Segmentation:** Sliced customer populations across relative 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month lookback windows to compare active buyers against dormant accounts.
* **B2B / Company-Level Aggregations:** Grouped activity metrics at the enterprise/company level to evaluate account-based transactional behavior.
* **Campaign Reach Evaluation:** Computed distinct customer reach metrics for targeted marketing campaigns across rolling timeframes (3, 6, and 12 months).
* **Operational Throughput Tracking:** Constructed time-series queries to monitor daily transaction volumes and platform activity spikes.

### 💡 Insight
Segmenting transaction data across dynamic lookback windows allows for automated churn prediction and campaign performance tracking. Grouping these metrics by partner company ensures actionable insights for B2B account management.
