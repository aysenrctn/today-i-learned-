# 📧 System Design: Interview Question Automation & Email Delivery

I researched the technical and financial feasibility of pre-interview question delivery systems to optimize the candidate experience.

### 🔍 Research & Reporting
* **Workflow Design:** Explored the automation of sending interview questions to users via email prior to their scheduled sessions.
* **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Evaluated various email service providers (ESPs) and automation tools, comparing their pricing models and reliability.
* **Pros & Cons:** Reported on the trade-offs between system-sent emails (efficiency) versus manual processes (personalization/cost-saving).
* **Team Sharing:** Presented a formal report to the team to decide on the most sustainable integration path.

### 💡 Insight
Every feature has a hidden cost—not just in development time, but in operational expenses. Conducting a thorough cost-benefit analysis before implementation is vital for the project's long-term sustainability.
