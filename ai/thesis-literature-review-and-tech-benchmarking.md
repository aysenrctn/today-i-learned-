# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence: Thesis Literature Review & Tech-Stack Justification

Today, I conducted a systematic academic literature review of 10–15 research papers aligned with my graduation thesis domain to analyze architectural choices, benchmark methodologies, and justify my proposed technology stack for academic advisement.

### 🚀 Key Technical Deliverables
* **Systematic Literature Review:** Screened and synthesized 10–15 domain-specific papers to identify prevailing deep learning architectures, metric learning strategies, and dataset handling techniques.
* **Comparative Technology Benchmarking:** Analyzed why preceding researchers selected specific frameworks, model backbones, and loss functions over alternatives, evaluating their algorithmic performance and constraints.
* **Architecture Justification:** Formulated a structured rationale justifying my chosen technology stack, contrasting its computational efficiency, feature extraction capability, and scalability against existing literature baselines.
* **Academic Synthesis Document:** Authored a synthesized briefing document summarizing comparative findings and architectural decisions for my faculty advisor review.

### 💡 Insight
Grounding engineering choices in a formal literature review transforms subjective technology selection into evidence-based systems design. Understanding why previous implementations succeeded or bottlenecked prevents redundant experimentation and establishes defensible architectural baselines.
