# 🌐 AI Systems: Multilingual Pipeline & Unit Cost Analysis

I researched the architectural requirements for supporting multilingual interviews in an AI-driven platform and conducted a unit economics analysis to estimate end-to-end execution costs per session.

---

### 📋 Key Highlights

* **Multilingual Pipeline Architecture:** Evaluated language model adaptation, localized Speech-to-Text (STT), and Text-to-Speech (TTS) integration to provide seamless multi-language conversational capabilities.
* **Unit Cost & Economics:** Calculated estimated operational costs per interview by breaking down LLM prompt/completion tokens, audio transcription processing, and speech synthesis durations.
* **Latency vs. Accuracy Trade-offs:** Analyzed the latency overhead introduced by localized speech models versus managed cloud APIs to maintain real-time conversational pacing.

---

### 💡 Insight

Designing production-ready AI systems requires balancing model capabilities with operational economics. Estimating per-session compute and API usage early helps prevent unexpected infrastructure overhead when expanding language capabilities.
