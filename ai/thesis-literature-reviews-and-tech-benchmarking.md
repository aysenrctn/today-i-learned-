# 🎓 Academic Research: Literature Review, Product Benchmarking & Tech Justification

Today, I conducted a systematic literature and product review to benchmark existing solutions, analyze domain-specific architectures, and document tech-stack justifications for my graduation project.

### 🚀 Key Technical Deliverables
* **Literature & Product Benchmarking:** Analyzed academic publications alongside existing commercial/open-source implementations to evaluate architectural patterns and feature sets.
* **Technology Justification:** Formulated technical rationales comparing framework performance, computational cost, and algorithmic accuracy against project constraints.
* **Documentation & Proposal Prep:** Consolidated findings into a structured technical report summarizing methodology trade-offs and tech-stack justifications for faculty review.

### 💡 Insight
Grounding architectural choices in both literature precedents and real-world product benchmarks creates a defensible technology stack, preventing rework during the implementation phase of a thesis project.
