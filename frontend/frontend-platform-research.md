# 🔍 Frontend Pivot: Platform Research & Evaluation

Today, the team made a strategic decision to revise the frontend architecture. I conducted a comprehensive research phase to identify the most suitable platform for our new requirements.

### 🚀 Key Evaluation Criteria
* **Developer Velocity:** Assessing which framework/platform allows for faster prototyping and iteration.
* **Scalability & Ecosystem:** Investigating the availability of pre-built components and community support to accelerate development.
* **Integration Ease:** Evaluating how seamlessly the new frontend platform can communicate with our existing Dockerized backend and PostgreSQL database.
* **Performance:** Comparing rendering techniques (SSR vs. CSR) to ensure a smooth user experience for "TalentAI Pro".

### 💡 Insight
Choosing the right frontend platform is not just about personal preference; it's about aligning technical capabilities with project goals. Pivoting early based on research is often more efficient than struggling with a tool that doesn't fit the long-term vision.
